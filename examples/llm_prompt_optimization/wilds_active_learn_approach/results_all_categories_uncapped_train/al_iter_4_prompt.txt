    <DynamicRules>
        - **5 Stars (Excellent/Enthusiastic):**
            - **Personal Enthusiasm:** Expresses strong, personal delight ('loved it', 'amazing', 'perfect', 'best ever'). Can also be shown by intensely positive descriptors ('gripping', 'brilliant') or a highly detailed, engaged summary with no complaints.
            - **Frictionless Experience:** For functional products, a brief, positive review stating it works as expected without issues ("Works great," "Well made," "Does the job") is 5 stars.
            - **Dismissed Negatives:** A negative point that is immediately dismissed or framed as a positive ("It's small, but that's what I wanted") still indicates a 5-star rating.

        - **4 Stars (Good/Satisfied):**
            - **Descriptive Praise (Media Default):** The default for positive media reviews using descriptive praise ('well-written', 'good plot', 'enjoyable') but lacking the personal, emotional enthusiasm of a 5-star review.
            - **Plot Summary + Positive:** A plot summary with a simple positive verdict ("good story"). This is 4 stars even if the summary uses positive adjectives like 'fascinating'.
            - **Mentioned Flaw:** Mentions a specific flaw but the overall sentiment remains clearly positive ("I enjoyed it, but..."). This is different from a 3-star review where pros and cons are more balanced or praise is very weak.
            - **"4.5 stars":** A review mentioning "4.5 stars" should be rated 4.

        - **3 Stars (Okay/Mixed):**
            - **Significant Flaw:** A significant flaw ('unfinished story', 'lacked punch') balances out lukewarm praise ('decent', 'enjoyable enough').
            - **Praise for Genre, Not Book:** Expressing enjoyment for a genre or trope without evaluating the specific book is 3 stars, even with words like 'glad to see'.
            - **Conditional Recommendation:** Conditionally positive reviews ("Good if you're in the mood for a light romance.").

        - **2 Stars (Poor/Disappointed):** Expresses clear disappointment. The product has major flaws or failed to meet basic expectations. The negative aspects clearly dominate any minor positives.

        - **1 Star (Terrible/Angry):** Expresses anger, disgust, or regret. The product is a failure, useless, broken, or a scam. A strong warning to others not to buy.
    </DynamicRules>
</System>
