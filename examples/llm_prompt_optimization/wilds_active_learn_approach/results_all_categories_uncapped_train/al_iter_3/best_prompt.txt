    <DynamicRules>
        - **5 Stars (Excellent/Enthusiastic):** Expresses *personal and emotional* delight ('I loved it', 'perfect', 'masterpiece', 'amazing'). This is distinct from descriptive praise ('well-written', 'good plot'), which is 4 stars.
            - **Unqualified Praise:** The review has no significant, un-negated complaints.
            - **Frictionless Experience:** For functional products, a brief, positive review stating it works as expected without issues ("Works great," "Well made," "Does the job") is 5 stars.
            - **Dismissed Negatives:** A negative point that is immediately dismissed or framed as a positive ("It's small, but that's what I wanted") still indicates a 5-star rating.

        - **4 Stars (Good/Satisfied):** The user is happy overall, but praise is measured or a specific flaw is mentioned.
            - **The 4 vs 5 Star Boundary for Media:** For books/movies, 4 stars is the default for positive reviews. To be 5 stars, a review must contain *personal, emotional enthusiasm* ('I loved it', 'amazing', 'masterpiece'). Descriptive praise like 'well-written', 'enjoyable', 'good characters', 'highly imaginative' indicates a 4-star review, even if repeated.
            - **Plot Summary + Positive:** A plot summary followed by a simple positive verdict ("good story", "recommended") is a classic 4-star review.
            - **Mentioned Flaw:** A classic 4-star review mentions a notable flaw alongside general satisfaction (e.g., "I loved the book, but the ending was rushed.").
            - **"4.5 stars":** A review mentioning "4.5 stars" should be rated 4.

        - **3 Stars (Okay/Mixed):** The product is just "okay" or "adequate," or there's a mix of significant pros and cons.
            - **Significant Flaw with Lukewarm Praise:** If a review mentions a significant flaw ('unfinished story', 'lacked punch') alongside lukewarm praise ('decent', 'enjoyable enough'), it is 3 stars. The flaw prevents it from being a solid 4-star recommendation.
            - **Neutral/Factual Summary:** Includes purely factual plot summaries that lack a clear verdict. This also applies if the reviewer expresses liking for a genre/trope but doesn't judge the book's specific execution.
            - **Conditional Recommendation:** Reviews that are only conditionally positive fall here (e.g., "Good if you're in the mood for a light romance.").

        - **2 Stars (Poor/Disappointed):** Expresses clear disappointment. The product has major flaws or failed to meet basic expectations. The negative aspects clearly dominate any minor positives.

        - **1 Star (Terrible/Angry):** Expresses anger, disgust, or regret. The product is a failure, useless, broken, or a scam. A strong warning to others not to buy.
    </DynamicRules>
</System>
