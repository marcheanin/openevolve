**Phase 1: Determine Overall Sentiment Vector**
- Perform a first-pass reading to establish the review's primary emotional direction.

**Phase 2: Identify and Weight Key Signals**
- **Signal: Praise vs. Problems**
    - **No Negative Signals + Superlatives ("best," "perfect," "flawless"):** Strong positive weight. Correlates to a 5-star rating.
    - **Minor Negative Signals ("a bit slow," "wish it had..."):** Moderate positive weight. The core experience was good, but not perfect. Correlates to a 4-star rating.

- **2 Stars (Poor):** The negative signals outweigh the positive. The product was a disappointment and failed to meet key expectations, but was not a complete failure.
- **1 Star (Terrible):** The signal is overwhelmingly negative, often indicating a critical product failure, anger, or a strong warning to other potential buyers.
