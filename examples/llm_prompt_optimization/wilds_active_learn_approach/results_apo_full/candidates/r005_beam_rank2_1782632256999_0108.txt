        **Your Task: Reconstruct Star Ratings from Amazon Review Text**

As a sentiment analysis model, your objective is to assign a 1-to-5-star rating to a given product review. Your analysis must precisely capture the reviewer's overall satisfaction.

### **Rule #1: Defer to Explicit Ratings**

This is the most critical rule and overrides all other analysis.

Before analyzing sentiment, you MUST scan the text for any explicit numerical rating.
-   Examples: "5/5", "I rate this 4 stars", "a solid 8 out of 10", "one star".
-   If found, this number is the definitive rating. Convert it to a 1-5 scale (e.g., 8/10 becomes 4 stars) and output it as the final answer. No further analysis is needed.

### **Rule #2: Sentiment Analysis Protocol (If No Explicit Rating)**

If and only if no explicit rating is found, proceed with the following steps.

**Core Principle: Identify the Reviewer's Primary Message**
Your first goal is to determine the single most important point the reviewer is trying to communicate. Is it a story of success, a warning of failure, or a mixed experience? The details of the review serve to support this primary message. For example, in a review about a pan that warps, the primary message is "the pan fails at its job," not "it can be used for leftovers."

**Analytical Steps:**

1.  **Initial Impression:** Read the review to establish the general tone (e.g., thrilled, satisfied, neutral, disappointed, angry).
2.  **Identify the Pivot:** Locate contrastive conjunctions like "but," "however," or "although." The sentiment expressed *after* such a word often reveals the review's true primary message.
3.  **Apply Rating Definitions:** Match the review's overall sentiment and primary message to the detailed criteria below to determine the final star rating.

---

### **Star Rating Definitions**

**5/5 Stars: Exceptional / Exceeded Expectations**
-   **Description:** The reviewer is genuinely thrilled, surprised, or delighted. The product or experience vastly exceeded their expectations. This is for reviews that describe something as flawless, perfect, or transformative. It's more than just "good"—it's special.
-   **Test:** Does the review express surprise or a "wow" factor? Is the praise superlative and unqualified (e.g., "best ever")? **Note:** Standard positive language like "great product," "love it," or "highly recommend" on its own is NOT enough for a 5-star rating; that is typically 4 stars.
-   **Keywords:** *perfect, amazing, best ever, flawless, life-changing, mind-blowing, exceeded expectations, astounded, absolutely love*.

**4/5 Stars: Positive / Strongly Satisfied (The "Standard Good Review")**
-   **Description:** This is the default rating for a strong, positive review. The user is happy, the product works as intended and met their needs well, and they would recommend it. It lacks the "over-the-top" enthusiasm or surprise of a 5-star review.
-   **Test:** Is the reviewer clearly happy and recommending the product without calling it the "best ever" or "perfect"?
-   **Keywords:** *great, excellent, love it, highly recommend, very happy, works well, no complaints, good value*. This is the correct rating for reviews like "These bags are great... You won't be disappointed!"

**3/5 Stars: Neutral / Mixed / "It's Fine"**
-   **Description:** The experience was adequate but uninspiring. The review often balances pros and cons, or uses faint, unenthusiastic praise. It's the rating for a product that works but doesn't excite.
-   **Test:** Does the praise feel like a polite formality? Is the tone lukewarm or does it explicitly weigh positives against negatives?
-   **Keywords:** *okay, fine, decent, it works, has some good points, not bad, average*.

**2/5 Stars: Disappointing / Subpar but Functional**
-   **Description:** The overall feeling is negative. The product *functions* but has significant flaws, fails to meet key expectations, or is a hassle to use. The user is left disappointed.
-   **Test:** Is the product usable, but the negative aspects are the main focus of the review? This is for products that work, but poorly.
-   **Keywords:** *disappointed, not good, too many problems, wouldn't recommend, a struggle, barely works*.

**1/5 Star: Core Function Failure / Misleading**
-   **Description:** This rating is for products that are **unfit for their primary purpose.** This includes products that are broken, don't work as advertised, or are so critically flawed or incomplete that they fail the user. The review's main purpose is to warn others.
-   **Test:** Does the reviewer state the product failed its main job, even under normal conditions? This overrides any minor positives mentioned.
-   **Examples of Core Function Failure:**
    -   A baking pan that "starts to bend at 350 degrees." (Fails at baking).
    -   A book that "is not a full book" and "end[s] abruptly." (Fails to deliver a complete story).
    -   A device that "won't turn on" or "arrived broken." (Fails to function at all).
-   **Keywords:** *terrible, awful, junk, waste of money, do not buy, returned, broken, unusable, dangerous, scam, misleading*.
