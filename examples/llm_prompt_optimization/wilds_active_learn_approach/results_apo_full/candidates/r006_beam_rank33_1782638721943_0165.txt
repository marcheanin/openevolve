        **Your Task: Reconstruct Star Ratings from Amazon Review Text**

As a sentiment analysis model, your objective is to assign a 1-to-5-star rating to a given product review. Your analysis must precisely capture the reviewer's overall satisfaction, enthusiasm, and final verdict.

### **Rule #1: Defer to Explicit Ratings (Highest Priority)**

This rule is absolute and overrides all other analysis.

Before analyzing sentiment, you MUST scan the text for any explicit numerical rating.
-   Examples: "5/5", "I rate this 4 stars", "a solid 8 out of 10", "one star".
-   If found, this number is the definitive rating. Convert it to a 1-5 scale (e.g., 8/10 becomes 4 stars) and output it as the final answer. No further analysis is needed.

### **Rule #2: Sentiment Analysis (If No Explicit Rating)**

If no explicit rating is found, determine the rating by matching the review's primary message and overall tone to the definitions below.

**Guiding Principle: Identify the Reviewer's Final Verdict and Enthusiasm Level**
Your goal is to understand the reviewer's conclusive opinion. Is the praise unqualified and enthusiastic? Is it solid but standard? Is it mixed, weighing pros and cons? Or is it a clear warning? Details in the review support this final verdict.

**Crucial Note on Context:** Interpret words and phrases based on the surrounding text. For example, in a glowing review that says "The dynamics, the drama, the love story...all right," the phrase "all right" is a colloquialism for "perfectly executed," not lukewarm praise.

---

### **Star Rating Definitions**

**5/5 Stars: Exceptional / Deeply Impressed**
-   **Description:** The reviewer is genuinely delighted, surprised, or deeply impressed. The product or experience was memorable and exceeded expectations, or was seen as flawless for their needs. This rating is for praise that is comprehensive and enthusiastic. It's more than just "good"—it's special.
-   **Test:** Does the reviewer seem "wowed" or convey a sense of genuine excitement? Is the praise superlative, comprehensive, and without significant reservation?
-   **Note:** This rating does NOT require specific keywords like "perfect" or "best ever." A review like "I enjoyed this all the way through. The characters were wonderfully written" is a 5-star review because the praise is strong, specific, and unqualified.
-   **Indicators:** *perfect, amazing, best ever, flawless, life-changing, mind-blowing, exceeded expectations, astounded, wonderfully, fantastic, couldn't be happier*.

**4/5 Stars: Positive / Strongly Satisfied (The "Standard Great Review")**
-   **Description:** This is the default rating for a strong, positive review. The user is happy, the product works as intended, and they would recommend it. It lacks the "over-the-top" enthusiasm or "wow" factor of a 5-star review.
-   **Test:** Is the reviewer clearly happy and satisfied, but the praise uses standard positive language?
-   **Note:** This is the correct home for most strong positive reviews.
-   **Keywords:** *great, excellent, love it, highly recommend, very happy, works well, no complaints, good value, awesomeness*. A review like "Excellent work. I loved the story line and characters" is a classic 4-star review.

**3/5 Stars: Neutral / Mixed / "It's Fine"**
-   **Description:** The experience was adequate but uninspiring. The review **explicitly balances significant pros and cons**, or the praise is faint and unenthusiastic. It's for a product that works but doesn't excite.
-   **Test:** Does the review read like a pro/con list? Is the praise qualified (e.g., "good for the price," "an interesting early effort but could be fleshed out")?
-   **Keywords:** *okay, fine, decent, it works, has some good points, not bad, average, mixed feelings*.

**2/5 Stars: Disappointing / Subpar but Functional**
-   **Description:** The overall feeling is negative. The product technically *functions* but has significant flaws, fails to meet key expectations, or is a hassle. The user is left disappointed and the negative aspects are the main focus.
-   **Test:** Is the product usable, but the review's primary message is about its significant failings?
-   **Keywords:** *disappointed, not good, too many problems, wouldn't recommend, a struggle, barely works*.

**1/5 Star: Core Function Failure / Misleading**
-   **Description:** This rating is for products that are **unfit for their primary purpose.** This includes products that are broken upon arrival, don't work as advertised, are dangerously flawed, or fail at their most basic function. The review's main purpose is to warn other buyers.
-   **Test:** Does the reviewer state the product failed its main job? This overrides any minor positives mentioned.
-   **Examples of Core Function Failure:** A pan that warps at normal cooking temperatures; a book that is incomplete; a device that won't turn on.
-   **Keywords:** *terrible, awful, junk, waste of money, do not buy, returned, broken, unusable, dangerous, scam, misleading*.
