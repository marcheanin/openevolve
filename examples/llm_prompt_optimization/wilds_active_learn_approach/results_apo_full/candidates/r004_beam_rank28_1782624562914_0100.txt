### Variation 1: The Technical & Direct Approach

**Hierarchical Rating Criteria (Evaluate in order):**

**Class 1: Deal-Breaker / Critical Failure**
*   **Conditions:** The review indicates the product was defective, broken, or unusable; expresses extreme anger or a feeling of being deceived (e.g., "scam," "garbage," "hate it"); or serves primarily as a warning to other potential buyers.
*   **Action:** Assign **1 Star**.

**Class 2: Significant Disappointment**
*   **Conditions:** The review is predominantly negative, highlighting major flaws that overshadow any positives. **Class 4: Positive with Minor Reservations**
*   **Conditions:** The review is overwhelmingly positive but mentions one or two small, non-critical issues. The overall tone is one of satisfaction and the user would recommend the product.

**Class 5: Enthusiastic Endorsement**
*   **Conditions:** The review is glowing, contains specific praise, and offers no notable criticism. The user is highly satisfied and recommends the product without reservation.

### Variation 2: The "Waterfall" Method

This version uses a "waterfall" metaphor to emphasize the top-down, sequential nature of the task.

**Instruction:**

Your task is to assign a 1-to-5 star rating to the following product review. Use the "Waterfall Rating Method" described below. You must start at the top (1 Star) and work your way down. The first category that accurately captures the review's main point is the one you must choose. Do not proceed further down the list once a match is found.

**The Waterfall Rating Method:**

**1-Star Filter (Abject Failure):**
*   Does the review describe a catastrophic flaw, product failure, or intense anger?
*   Is its main purpose to warn others away (e.g., "AVOID AT ALL COSTS")?
*   If yes, assign **1 Star**. If no, proceed to the next filter.

**2-Star Filter (Major Disappointment):**
*   Is the review clearly negative, where flaws outweigh any strengths?
*   Does the user express dissatisfaction or state they gave up on the product out of boredom?
*   If yes, assign **2 Stars**. If no, proceed to the next filter.

**3-Star Filter (Ambivalent or Average):**
*   Is the review a mix of good and bad points?
*   Does the user use neutral language like "it was okay" or give a recommendation with a big "but"?
*   Does it express disappointment compared to a previous version (e.g., "not as good as the first")?
*   If yes, assign **3 Stars**. If no, proceed to the next filter.

**4-Star Filter (Generally Great):**
*   Is the review very positive, with only minor, non-deal-breaker complaints?
*   Does the user seem satisfied and likely to recommend it?
*   If yes, assign **4 Stars**. If no, proceed to the next filter.

**5-Star Filter (Flawless Praise):**
*   Is the review entirely positive, with specific, enthusiastic praise and no critiques?
*   If yes, assign **5 Stars**.

### Variation 3: The Concise & Imperative Approach

This version is shorter, using direct commands and a simplified structure.

**Instruction:**

Evaluate the sentiment of the provided review and assign a star rating from 1 to 5. You must follow the rules below in sequence. Stop and assign the rating as soon as a rule's criteria are met.

**Rating Rules (Apply the first one that fits):**

*   **1 Star if:** The review expresses anger, describes a non-working or dangerous product, or explicitly warns others not to buy. The user feels cheated or gave up in frustration.
    *   *Examples: "arrived broken," "complete garbage," "DO NOT BUY."*

*   **2 Stars if:** The review is negative and shows clear disappointment. The product's weaknesses are the main focus, and any positives are dismissed. The user may have quit out of boredom.
    *   *Examples: "I didn't enjoy this," "the flaws were too distracting," "I couldn't finish it."*

*   **3 Stars if:** The review is mixed, with a balance of pros and cons. The user calls the product "average," "okay," or "good, but not great." The recommendation is conditional.
    *   *Examples: "fine for the price," "good for fans, but others should skip."*

*   **4 Stars if:** The review is strongly positive but mentions a minor flaw that doesn't ruin the experience. The user is satisfied overall.
    *   *Examples: "loved it, though the ending felt rushed," "great product, wish it came in more colors."*

*   **5 Stars if:** The review is purely positive and enthusiastic, with no complaints. The user's expectations were exceeded.
    *   *Examples: "perfect in every way," "an absolute masterpiece," "highly recommend."*
