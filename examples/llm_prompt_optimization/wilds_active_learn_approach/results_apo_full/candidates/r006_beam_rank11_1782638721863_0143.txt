        **Your Task: Reconstruct Star Ratings from Amazon Review Text**

As a sentiment analysis model, your objective is to assign a 1-to-5-star rating to a given product review. Your analysis must precisely capture the reviewer's overall satisfaction, considering the context of their expectations for the product's price and purpose.

### **Rule #1: Defer to Explicit Ratings**

This is the most critical rule and overrides all other analysis.

Before analyzing sentiment, you MUST scan the text for any explicit numerical rating.
-   Examples: "5/5", "I rate this 4 stars", "a solid 8 out of 10", "one star".
-   If found, this number is the definitive rating. Convert it to a 1-5 scale (e.g., 8/10 becomes 4 stars) and output it as the final answer. No further analysis is needed.

### **Rule #2: Sentiment Analysis Protocol (If No Explicit Rating)**

If and only if no explicit rating is found, proceed with the following steps.

**Core Principle: Determine Overall Satisfaction within Context**
Your goal is to understand how happy the reviewer is with their purchase, considering what they expected for the price and product type. A cheap mouse can be a 5-star purchase even if the reviewer acknowledges a more expensive one is better. Your job is to capture the reviewer's final verdict for the product *in its own context*.

**Analytical Steps:**

1.  **Holistic Impression:** Read the entire review to get the overall tone. Is the reviewer delighted, satisfied, ambivalent, disappointed, or angry?
2.  **Identify Key Statements:** Pinpoint the most definitive statements of praise or criticism. Pay special attention to contrastive words ("but," "however," "although") as the clause that follows often reveals the true takeaway.
3.  **Evaluate Context and Nuance:** This is a critical step. Ask:
    *   **Is there a comparison?** If the reviewer compares the product unfavorably to a more expensive one but still strongly recommends it *for its price or purpose* (e.g., "The pro model is better, but for a budget option, this is fantastic"), this is still a highly positive signal.
    *   **What is the final verdict?** The concluding sentences often summarize the reviewer's definitive opinion and carry the most weight.
4.  **Apply Rating Definitions:** Match your holistic, contextual analysis to the definitions below.

---

### **Star Rating Definitions**

**5/5 Stars: Enthusiastic Recommendation / Perfect for its Purpose**
-   **Description:** The reviewer is thoroughly impressed and gives a wholehearted, enthusiastic recommendation. The product met or exceeded their expectations *for its intended purpose, price point, or category*. It doesn't have to be objectively "perfect," but it's a perfect fit for the reviewer's needs. This is for reviews that convey delight, strong positive emotion, or a feeling of "great value."
-   **Test:** Is the reviewer's final verdict an enthusiastic "yes" for a specific audience or use case? Does the review contain strong emotional praise ("wonderfully written," "can't wait for more")? **Crucially, acknowledging a better, more expensive alternative exists does NOT disqualify a 5-star rating if the recommendation for *this* product remains enthusiastic.**
-   **Keywords/Examples:** *fantastic, amazing, outstanding, perfect for my needs, excellent value, couldn't be happier, wonderfully written*. A review like "I like the M705 better, but for a travel mouse, this one is extremely nice" is a 5-star review.

**4/5 Stars: Positive / Satisfied with Minor Reservations**
-   **Description:** A strong, positive review for a product that works well and is recommended, but the reviewer notes one or more specific, non-trivial drawbacks that prevent it from being a perfect experience. The core function is great, but something holds it back.
-   **Test:** Does the review have a "Great product, BUT..." structure, where the "but" introduces a real flaw (not just a contextual comparison)? The overall sentiment is clearly positive, but lacks the unreserved enthusiasm of a 5-star review.
-   **Keywords/Examples:** *great, very good, works well, happy with it, recommend with caveats*. "I enjoyed this all the way through" is positive, but lacks the emotional punch for a 5-star rating, making it a solid 4.

**3/5 Stars: Neutral / Mixed / "It's Fine"**
-   **Description:** The experience was adequate but uninspiring. The review explicitly balances significant pros and cons, with neither side winning out. It's the rating for a product that works but doesn't excite, or for entertainment that is enjoyable but forgettable.
-   **Test:** Is the final takeaway "it's okay," "it does the job," or "good, not great"? Does the review spend equal time on positives and negatives?
-   **Keywords/Examples:** *okay, fine, decent, average, it works, has some good points, not bad but not great*. A review like `"White Squall" is no masterpiece, but it serves as terrific entertainment` is a classic 3-star review, balancing a major critique ("no masterpiece") with a solid positive ("terrific entertainment").

**2/5 Stars: Disappointing / Subpar but Functional**
-   **Description:** The overall feeling is negative. The product *functions* but has significant flaws, fails to meet key expectations, or is a hassle to use. The user is left disappointed.
-   **Test:** Is the product usable, but the negative aspects are the main focus of the review? This is for products that work, but poorly.
-   **Keywords:** *disappointed, not good, too many problems, wouldn't recommend, a struggle, barely works*.

**1/5 Star: Core Function Failure / Misleading**
-   **Description:** This rating is for products that are **unfit for their primary purpose.** This includes products that are broken, don't work as advertised, or are so critically flawed or incomplete that they fail the user. The review's main purpose is to warn others.
-   **Test:** Does the reviewer state the product failed its main job? This overrides any minor positives.
-   **Examples of Core Function Failure:** A pan that "starts to bend at 350 degrees." A book that "is not a full book." A device that "won't turn on."
-   **Keywords:** *terrible, awful, junk, waste of money, do not buy, returned, broken, unusable, dangerous, scam*.
