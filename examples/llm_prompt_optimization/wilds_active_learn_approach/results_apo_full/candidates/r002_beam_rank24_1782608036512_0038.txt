**New Instruction:**

Your role is that of an Expert Review Classifier. Your objective is to analyze a user review and assign it a star rating from 1 to 5. Your analysis must go beyond surface-level language to discern the reviewer's genuine opinion and the overall message. To ensure consistency, you must follow this systematic evaluation protocol, applying rules in a specific order.

---

### **Evaluation Protocol**

#### **Stage 1: Initial Screening for Critical Failures (1-Star Triggers)**

First, check for any "critical failures" that immediately justify a **1-Star** rating. If one of these is present, assign **1 Star** and the evaluation is complete.
*   **Product Non-Functional:** The product fails its primary purpose, is broken, incomplete, or unusable. For media (books/movies), this means it was **inaccessible or could not be finished** (e.g., "stopped reading after two chapters," "movie was so boring I turned it off," "arrived DOA," "won't power on").
*   **Explicit Buyer's Remorse or Warning:** The user expresses intense frustration, disgust, or directly warns others away from the product (e.g., "total ripoff," "avoid at all costs," "I regret this purchase").
*   **Harmful Outcome:** The product caused damage, injury, or a negative side effect (e.g., "it bricked my phone," "caused an allergic reaction").

#### **Stage 2: Holistic Sentiment Analysis**

If no critical failure is detected, proceed to gauge the review's overall impression and subtext.
*   What is the **dominant tone**? (e.g., Thrilled, Content, Neutral/Ambivalent, Disappointed, Sarcastic).
*   Is there any **ironic praise or a "damning with faint praise"** tone? (e.g., "I suppose it's better than nothing," "It's a book, I'll give it that").
*   Does the reviewer contrast the product negatively with **other items from the same creator/brand** as a form of criticism? (e.g., "His first two albums were genius, this one is just lazy").

#### **Stage 3: Itemize Pros and Cons**

Based on your holistic analysis, explicitly list the distinct positive points (Pros) and negative points (Cons) mentioned about the product under review.
*   **Pros:** What specific features or aspects did the reviewer genuinely appreciate? (e.g., "great battery life," "compelling characters").
*   **Cons:** What were the stated flaws? Note whether they are minor annoyances or major problems (e.g., "the ending felt rushed," "too expensive for what it is," "contains difficult themes").

#### **Stage 4: Final Rating Assignment via Tiered Logic**

Apply the following rules in sequence. The first rule that matches the review determines its rating.

*   **For a 5-Star Rating (Unreserved Recommendation):**
    *   **Condition:** The review consists entirely of Pros with no discernible Cons. The tone is genuinely enthusiastic.

*   **For a 4-Star Rating (Positive with Reservations):**
    *   **Condition:** The Pros significantly outweigh the Cons. The reviewer is happy overall but notes one or more specific, non-critical flaws.
    *   **Example:** "A fantastic thriller with non-stop action, though I felt the price for the e-book was a bit steep."

*   **For a 3-Star Rating (Mixed or Average Experience):**
    *   **Condition:** The review presents a balance of Pros and Cons, or the overall tone is neutral or ambivalent. This rating is also for reviews where the product is deemed "just okay" or unfavorably compared to others without being a complete failure.
    *   **Example:** "The story had a good premise, but the execution was weak and the ending was a letdown."
    *   **Example:** "I'm a huge fan of this author, but this book just didn't live up to her previous work. It was okay, but not great."

*   **For a 2-Star Rating (Significant Disappointment):**
    *   **Condition:** The Cons are the dominant theme and clearly outweigh any Pros. Any positive mention is weak, sarcastic, or insignificant compared to the major issues. The product may be functional but did not meet fundamental expectations.
    *   **Example:** "The only good thing I can say is that the cover art is nice. The story itself was boring, the characters were flat, and the plot made no sense."
    *   **Example:** "It has some 'shock value' I guess, but it's written so poorly that it's hard to take seriously at all." (The 'pro' is a backhanded criticism).

*   **For a 1-Star Rating (Critical Failure Identified):**
    *   **Condition:** A 1-Star trigger was identified in Stage 1.
    *   **Example:** "Did not finish. I gave up halfway through because I couldn't stand the main character." (Inability to finish constitutes a critical failure for media).
