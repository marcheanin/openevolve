**Core Philosophy: Prioritize Emotional Intensity and Overall Success**
Your objective is to determine the reviewer's level of excitement and whether the product succeeded or failed at its primary function. The *degree* of satisfaction is what separates different star levels.

**Analysis Methodology:**

1.  **Initial Tone Assessment:** Begin with a full read-through to grasp the review's overall feeling. Is it thrilled, pleased, ambivalent, frustrated, or furious?

2.  **Identify High-Impact "Lock-In" Signals:** Search for decisive phrases that reveal the core outcome. These often serve as definitive indicators for a specific rating, overriding other conflicting language.
    *   **Critical Failure Signal (1-Star Lock):** A statement that the reviewer **abandoned the product** out of dislike (e.g., "**couldn't finish the book**," "**stopped watching**," "**gave up on it**") is a powerful signal for a **1-star** rating. It indicates total failure.
    *   **Profound Connection Signal (5-Star Lock):** Phrases expressing deep gratitude or a lasting personal impact (e.g., "**Thank you for this story**," "**This changed how I see...**," "**It will stay with me forever**") signal a transformative experience worthy of a **5-star** rating.

3.  **Analyze Tonal Shifts and Qualifying Language:** Pay close attention to contrastive words ("but," "however") and phrases that "cap" the praise ("good, not great," "it's fine," "decent for the price"). **Detailed Rating Definitions:**

-   **5 Stars (Exceptional / Transformative):**
    -   **Concept:** The experience was flawless or deeply meaningful. The product either performed perfectly or created a powerful, memorable emotional response. It went far beyond expectations.
    -   **Indicators:** Unreserved enthusiasm ("best ever," "perfect," "masterpiece," "life-changing"). Or, a deep personal connection expressed with gratitude or emotion. This is the "no reservations" recommendation.

-   **4 Stars (Strongly Positive / Great):**
    -   **Concept:** The default rating for a very positive review. The product works excellently and the customer is happy and would recommend it, but the praise lacks the "wow" factor or profound emotional depth of a 5-star review.
    -   **Indicators:** Strong, positive descriptors like "excellent," "fantastic," "loved it," "highly recommend." The review is clearly a strong endorsement.

-   **3 Stars (Average / Mixed / "It's Fine"):**
    -   **Concept:** The experience was adequate but uninspiring. This rating represents a balanced scorecard of pros and cons, or faint praise that suggests the product is merely competent.
    -   **Indicators:** Ambivalent language like "it was okay," "decent," "good, not great," or a list of positives and negatives that cancel each other out. The feeling is one of mild satisfaction or indifference.

-   **2 Stars (Negative / Disappointing):**
    -   **Concept:** The product has significant flaws and failed to meet expectations. The negativity is the dominant theme of the review, even if minor positive points are mentioned begrudgingly.
    -   **Indicators:** Words like "disappointed," "letdown," "too many problems." The tone is often one of frustration, dismissal, or sarcasm.

-   **1 Star (Total Failure / Warning):**
    -   **Concept:** The product was unusable, a complete waste of time/money, or so bad it prompted anger. This rating serves as a strong warning to other potential buyers.
    -   **Indicators:** Harsh terms like "awful," "terrible," "garbage," "don't buy," "returned it." Critically, this includes the **"abandoned experience"** signal ("did not finish," "couldn't get through it").
