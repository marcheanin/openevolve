### **Phase 2: Sequential Analysis (If No Literal Rating Found)**

**Gate A: The Catastrophic Failure (1 Star)**
*   **Condition:** The review describes a product that is non-functional, fundamentally defective, or a complete betrayal of its advertised purpose. The tone is often a warning to other potential buyers.
*   **Indicators:**
    *   Total non-functionality: "DOA," "won't charge," "fell apart instantly."

**Gate B: Unreserved Enthusiasm (5 Stars)**
*   **Condition:** The review expresses overwhelming positivity and delight, suggesting the product dramatically surpassed the user's expectations. Mere satisfaction is not enough.
*   **Indicators:**
    *   Superlative language indicating astonishment: "absolute best," "game-changer," "flawless," "literally perfect," "mind-blowing."
    *   Expressions of surprise and deep satisfaction: "I was skeptical, but wow," "exceeded my wildest expectations."
*   **Critical Distinction:** This gate is NOT triggered by standard positive feedback. Words like "great," "excellent," or "love it" on their own are insufficient. The review must convey an element of ecstatic surprise or declare the product to be in a class of its own.
*   **If Condition Met: Output is 5. Stop.**

**Gate C: Significant Dissatisfaction (2 Stars)**
*   **Condition:** The product is functional but is plagued by major shortcomings that cause significant frustration or disappointment. The user regrets the purchase and would not recommend it.
*   **Indicators:**
    *   Focus on persistent problems: "buggy," "frustrating," "has major design flaws."
    *   Expressions of disappointment: "let down," "not what I was hoping for," "expected much better."
    *   Clear non-endorsement: "would not buy again," "look elsewhere."
*   **If Condition Met: Output is 2. Stop.**

**Gate D: Ambivalent or Conditional Satisfaction (3 Stars)**
*   **Condition:** The reviewer's feelings are mixed, or their praise is heavily qualified. The product is seen as adequate but not impressive.
*   **Indicators:**
    *   A clear balance of pros and cons is discussed.
    *   Lukewarm language: "it's okay," "gets the job done," "fine," "decent."
    *   **Qualified Praise (Strong Signal):** Praise is limited by a specific context.

        *   By Use-Case: "works fine *for simple tasks*."
        *   By Audience: "a must-have *for collectors of this genre*." (This praises its niche appeal, not its universal quality).
*   **If Condition Met: Output is 3. Stop.**

**Gate E: The Standard Positive Review (4 Stars)**
*   **Condition:** If a review is clearly positive but did not meet the high bar for "Unreserved Enthusiasm" (Gate B), it defaults to this category. The product works well and the customer is happy.
*   **Description:** This is the catch-all for a satisfied customer whose expectations were met or slightly exceeded, but who was not "blown away."
*   **Indicators:** The review contains the positive words that were excluded from Gate B: "great product," "excellent," "love it," "works perfectly," "highly recommend."
*   **The output is 4.**
