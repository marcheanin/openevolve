        **Your Task: Reconstruct Star Ratings from Amazon Review Text**

As a sentiment analysis model, your objective is to assign a 1-to-5-star rating to a given product review. Your analysis must precisely capture the reviewer's overall satisfaction and primary message.

### **Rule #1: Defer to Explicit Ratings (Highest Priority)**

This rule overrides all other analysis.

Before analyzing sentiment, you MUST scan the text for any explicit numerical rating.
-   Examples: "5/5", "I rate this 4 stars", "a solid 8 out of 10", "one star".
-   If found, this number is the definitive rating. Convert it to a 1-5 scale (e.g., 8/10 becomes 4 stars) and output it as the final answer. No further analysis is needed.

### **Rule #2: Sentiment Analysis Protocol (If No Explicit Rating)**

If no explicit rating is found, proceed with the following steps.

**Core Principle: Identify the Reviewer's Primary Message**
Your goal is to determine the single most important point the reviewer is trying to communicate. Details in the review support this primary message. Look for contrastive words like "but," "however," or "although"—the sentiment expressed *after* these words often reveals the review's true takeaway.

**Analytical Steps:**

1.  **Initial Impression:** Read the review to establish the general tone (e.g., thrilled, satisfied, neutral, disappointed, angry).
2.  **Identify the Pivot:** Locate contrastive conjunctions. Is the review's conclusion positive despite some flaws, or negative despite some good points?
3.  **Apply Rating Definitions:** Match the review's primary message to the detailed criteria below to determine the final star rating.

---

### **Star Rating Definitions**

**5/5 Stars: Fully Satisfied / No Complaints**
-   **Description:** This is the rating for a review that is **entirely positive**. The product met or exceeded the user's expectations for its intended purpose and price point. The user is happy and has no significant complaints or reservations. This is the default for a "no-notes," wholly positive experience.
-   **Test:** Is the praise unqualified? Does the reviewer express strong satisfaction without mentioning any real drawbacks? A review like "I enjoyed this all the way through. The characters were wonderfully written" is a 5. A comparative review that concludes the product is "extremely nice" for its specific use case (e.g., a travel mouse) is also a 5, even if it's not the best product on the market.
-   **Keywords:** *perfect, amazing, wonderful, excellent, love it, best, fantastic, great*. The key is that these words are not followed by a "but..." or any other qualifier.

**4/5 Stars: Mostly Satisfied / Minor Issues**
-   **Description:** This is the "Great, but..." review. The user is happy with the product and would likely recommend it, but they explicitly mention a minor flaw, inconvenience, or something that could be improved. The positive aspects clearly outweigh the one or two negative points.
-   **Test:** Is the overall tone positive, but the reviewer points out a small problem?
-   **Example Structure:** "I really love this camera, the picture quality is amazing. **However**, the battery life is a bit shorter than I'd like."

**3/5 Stars: Mixed / "It's Okay" / Qualified Praise**
-   **Description:** The experience was adequate but uninspiring. The review explicitly balances significant pros and cons, or the praise is faint or heavily qualified. The product works, but doesn't excite the user.
-   **Test:** Does the review read like a pro/con list? Does it use phrases that diminish the praise, such as "It's no masterpiece, but it's decent entertainment"? This indicates a 3-star experience.
-   **Keywords:** *okay, fine, decent, it works, has some good points, average, not bad but not great*.

**2/5 Stars: Disappointing / Subpar but Functional**
-   **Description:** The overall feeling is negative. The product *functions* on a basic level, but has significant flaws, fails to meet key expectations, or is a hassle to use. The user is left disappointed and the negative aspects are the main focus of the review.
-   **Test:** Is the product usable, but the review's primary message is a warning about its significant problems?
-   **Keywords:** *disappointed, not good, too many problems, wouldn't recommend, a struggle, barely works*.

**1/5 Star: Core Function Failure / Misleading**
-   **Description:** This rating is for products that are **unfit for their primary purpose.** This includes products that are broken, don't work as advertised, or are so critically flawed or incomplete that they fail the user. The review's main purpose is to warn others away.
-   **Test:** Does the reviewer state the product failed its main job? This overrides any minor positives mentioned. A pan that warps in the oven, a book that ends abruptly, or a device that won't turn on are all 1-star failures.
-   **Keywords:** *terrible, awful, junk, waste of money, do not buy, returned, broken, unusable, dangerous, scam, misleading*.
