    <DynamicRules>
        Here is a variation of the instruction that preserves the semantic meaning but alters the phrasing, structure, and tone.

**New Instruction:**

Your function is to analyze the text of an Amazon product review and assign it a 1-to-5 star rating. Your analysis should focus on capturing the subtle nuances of sentiment, enthusiasm, and specific language to deduce the most probable original rating.

**The Overriding Mandate: Prioritize Explicit Ratings**
First, you must meticulously scan the review for any explicit numerical rating. This includes phrases like "I rate this 4 stars," "a solid 8/10," or "3 out of 5." If such a rating is found, it is the definitive answer. You must convert it to the 1-5 star scale (e.g., 8/10 becomes 4 stars) and ignore all other sentiment analysis. This rule is absolute.

**If no explicit rating exists, proceed with the following classification framework, which is built on a key principle:**

**The 4-Star Baseline for Positive Feedback:**
Consider a **4-star rating as the standard** for any review that is clearly positive and enthusiastic. A review does not automatically get 5 stars just for using positive adjectives like "great," "excellent," or "fantastic." To earn a 5-star rating, the review must demonstrate an exceptional, superlative, or "perfect" experience that sets it apart from a merely strong recommendation.

**Classification Funnel:**

Start at the bottom and work your way up to determine the correct rating.

1.  **Identify Overtly Negative Reviews (1-2 Stars):**
    -   **1-Star (Total Failure):** The product was unusable, broken, a "scam," or provoked anger. The primary function failed completely. Keywords: "useless," "broken on arrival," "waste of money," "terrible," "do not buy." The user is warning others away.
    -   **2-Star (Significant Disappointment):** The product works but has major flaws and did not meet basic expectations. The overall feeling is negative and regretful. Keywords: "disappointing," "full of problems," "not what I expected," "wouldn't recommend."

2.  **Identify Neutral or Mixed Reviews (3 Stars):**
    -   **3-Star (Average / Lukewarm):** If not negative, check for a "meh" or "it's fine" sentiment. This rating is for reviews that balance pros and cons, express faint praise, or lack enthusiasm. The product is functional but uninspiring.
    -   **Key Indicators:** "it's okay," "decent," "not bad." Critically, polite but passionless phrases like "a good effort," "well-written," or "worth a read" (especially with qualifiers like "for the price") often signal a 3-star review.

3.  **Distinguish Between Strong and Exceptional Positive Reviews (4-5 Stars):**
    -   If the review is clearly positive, you must apply this final, strict differentiation.

    -   **The 5-Star Threshold (Exceptional & Unsurpassed):**
        To cross the threshold into a 5-star rating, a review must meet at least **one** of these high standards:
        -   **Superlative Claims:** It uses language like "the best I've ever used," "absolutely flawless," "unparalleled," or "in a class by itself."
        -   **Statements of Perfection:** The reviewer explicitly says there are "no negatives," "zero complaints," or that they "couldn't find a single thing wrong."
        -   **Profound Personal Impact (for creative works):** For media, it mentions a life-changing impact or deep gratitude, like "this book changed my perspective" or "I'll cherish this album forever."
        -   **Crucial Caveat:** A list of strong but standard positive words ("great," "awesome," "wonderful") is **insufficient** for a 5-star rating. It must be elevated by one of the criteria above.

    -   **The 4-Star Standard (Strongly Recommended):**
        This is the **default rating for a satisfied customer.** It is for reviews that are enthusiastic but not superlative. It's the home for strong endorsements like "highly recommend," "loved it," "excellent product," and "you won't regret it." A review that lists multiple positive features without claiming perfection is a quintessential 4-star review.
