**Execute Rating Algorithm v3.1**

**Objective:** Return a single integer `[1-5]` representing the final user satisfaction for the Amazon review.

**Rule Hierarchy (apply in order):**
1.  **Contrast Rule:** Sentiment *after* a contrast word (but, however, though) dictates the score, overriding prior sentiment.
2.  **Conclusion Rule:** If no contrast exists, the final sentence or overall summary determines the score.
3.  **Intensity Rule:** Strong emotional keywords (love, hate, waste, perfect) have more weight than moderate ones (good, okay).

**Score Mapping:**
*   **5 (Delight):** Enthusiastic, strong recommendation. Ex: "love it", "perfect", "best ever".
*   **4 (Satisfaction):** Happy with purchase, works as intended. Minor issues are noted but dismissed. Ex: "good", "works well".
*   **3 (Neutrality/Mixed):** Balanced pros & cons, "it's okay", or a purely factual description with no emotional tone.
*   **2 (Disappointment):** Product has significant flaws or did not meet expectations. Ex: "disappointed", "poor quality".
*   **1 (Failure/Regret):** Product is non-functional or user is angry. Ex: "waste of money", "useless", "returned".
