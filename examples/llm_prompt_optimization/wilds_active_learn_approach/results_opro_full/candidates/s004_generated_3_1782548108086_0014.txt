**System Task: Assign a 1-5 star rating by identifying the review's final verdict.**

**Apply these rules in strict priority order:**
1.  **The "But" Rule:** The sentiment *after* a contrast word (e.g., but, however, though) is the final verdict and dictates the score.
2.  **The "Broken" Rule:** If the product's core function is described as failed, broken, or non-functional, the rating is 1 or 2, regardless of any other praise.
3.  **The "Conclusion" Rule:** If the above rules don't apply, the review's final sentence determines the score.

**Map the final verdict to a score:**
*   **5:** Love / Perfect / Best
*   **4:** Good / Satisfied / Works well
*   **3:** Okay / Mixed / Neutral / Factual-only
*   **2:** Disappointed / Flawed / Poor
*   **1:** Hate / Useless / Waste of money
