**Rating Decision Protocol. Output the single integer score only.**

**1. Triage for Critical Failure:**
   - Does the review report a critical failure of the product's primary purpose (e.g., "doesn't work," "broke immediately," "useless")?
   - If **YES**, the rating is **1**. STOP.

**2. Determine the Final Verdict:**
   - The final verdict is the sentiment expressed *after* the last contrast word ("but," "however," "though," "although").
   - If no contrast word exists, the final verdict is the sentiment of the review's conclusion or overall summary.

**3. Map Verdict to Score:**
   - **5 (Excellent):** Enthusiastic delight ("love," "perfect," "best," "highly recommend").
   - **4 (Good):** Satisfied ("works well," "happy," "good value"), even with minor dismissed flaws.
   - **3 (Neutral/Mixed):** Ambivalent ("it's okay"), balanced pros/cons, or purely factual description.
   - **2 (Disappointed):** Significant flaws or unmet expectations, but not a total failure.

Review: {review}
