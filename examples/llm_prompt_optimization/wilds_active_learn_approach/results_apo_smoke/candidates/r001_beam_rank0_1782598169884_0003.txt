Follow this logic:

### Star Rating Definitions & Rules

**⭐ 1 Star: Fundamental Failure or No Value**
*   **Core Idea:** The user received no value or enjoyment. The product was unusable, deeply unenjoyable, or failed its primary purpose. The overall experience was a net negative.
*   **Key Distinction:** This rating is not just for angry rants. It also applies to polite but firmly negative reviews where the user clearly states they did not like it, couldn't use it, or wouldn't recommend it. The *outcome* is a total lack of value for the user.
*   **Signals:** "Did not enjoy," "hard time," "could not use," "waste of money," "returned it," "terrible," "worst," "product failure," "not to my taste" (when this is the final conclusion).

**⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Significant Disappointment**
*   **Core Idea:** The product worked but failed to meet key expectations, leaving the user clearly disappointed. There are major flaws, but it wasn't a complete and total failure.
*   **Key Distinction:** Unlike a 1-star review, the user might express a minor positive or acknowledge that the product *partially* works. The dominant feeling is disappointment, not total rejection.
*   **Signals:** "Disappointed," "not as good as I hoped," "major flaws," "had to force myself to finish," "it works, but..." (where the "but" introduces a significant problem).

**⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Mixed, Neutral, or Average**
*   **Core Idea:** The user has balanced feelings, or the experience was simply "average" with no strong emotions. It's the rating for "it's fine."
*   **Key Distinction:** Pros and cons are explicitly mentioned and seem to carry equal weight. It can also apply to purely informational or descriptive reviews that lack a strong emotional tone.
*   **Signals:** "Okay," "average," "has good and bad points," "it does the job," "not sure how I feel."

**⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Good, with Minor Reservations**
*   **Core Idea:** The user is clearly satisfied and feels the product is good, but they mention a specific, non-critical flaw or a minor reservation. This is the "Great, but..." category.
*   **Key Distinction:** Even if a review uses strong positive language ("love," "great," "enjoyed"), the presence of a clear, valid critique or a point of hesitation (e.g., "I didn't warm up to it at first," "the ending was a bit weak") caps the rating at 4 stars.
*   **Signals:** "Liked it a lot," "very good," "would recommend," "enjoyed," "great addition," followed by a minor complaint or critique.

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Excellent, No Reservations**
*   **Core Idea:** The user is enthusiastic and fully satisfied. The product met or exceeded all expectations.
*   **Key Distinction:** A 5-star review is overwhelmingly positive and does not contain any significant flaws or reservations. If a critique is mentioned, it's insignificant or immediately dismissed (e.g., "the box was dented but the product inside was perfect").
*   **Signals:** "Love," "perfect," "amazing," "best ever," "exceeded my expectations," "highly recommend," with no meaningful drawbacks mentioned.
