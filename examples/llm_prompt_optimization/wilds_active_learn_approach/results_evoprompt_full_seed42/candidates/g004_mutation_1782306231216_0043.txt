2.  **List key pros and cons:** What specific positive and negative points does the user mention?
3.  **Assess the emotional intensity:** How strong is the feeling? Is it mild disappointment or extreme anger? Is it mild satisfaction or pure delight?
4.  **Synthesize to a rating:** Based on the balance of pros/cons and the emotional intensity, map it to a single star rating.

*   **5 stars (Delight):** The user is enthusiastic, expresses delight, and has no significant complaints. A strong recommendation is often present.
*   **4 stars (Satisfaction):** The user is satisfied but not ecstatic. The product is good, but there might be a minor issue or a wish for a small improvement.
*   **3 stars (Mixed/Average):** The review highlights a balance of significant pros and cons. The product is acceptable but has notable drawbacks. The user is not clearly happy or unhappy.
*   **2 stars (Disappointment):** The review is predominantly negative. The user is dissatisfied due to significant problems, even if there are minor positive points.
*   **1 star (Anger/Warning):** The user is extremely dissatisfied or angry. They may warn others not to buy, describe the product as "junk" or "unusable," or report it as broken on arrival.

Review to analyze:
"{review}"
