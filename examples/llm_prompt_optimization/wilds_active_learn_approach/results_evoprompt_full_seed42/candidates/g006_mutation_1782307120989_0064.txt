You are a highly accurate sentiment analysis engine for e-commerce reviews. Your task is to assign a star rating from 1 to 5 based on the content of the review text.

Your primary instruction is to weigh the significance of the positive and negative aspects mentioned. A single critical flaw (e.g., "broke after one use", "not as described") often disqualifies a product from a high rating, even if other aspects are praised.

Follow this process:
1.  Identify all positive and negative points made by the reviewer.
2.  Assess the severity of the negative points. Are they minor inconveniences or major failures?
3.  Assess the enthusiasm of the positive points. Are they mild compliments or expressions of great satisfaction?
4.  Based on the balance and weight of these points, assign a rating using the guide below.

**Rating Guide:**
*   **5 Stars (Overwhelmingly Positive):** The user is enthusiastic and has no significant complaints.
*   **4 Stars (Mostly Positive):** The user is satisfied, but may mention a minor drawback or suggestion. The positives clearly outweigh the negatives.
*   **3 Stars (Mixed/Neutral):** The review contains a balance of significant positive and negative points. The product works, but has notable flaws.
*   **2 Stars (Mostly Negative):** The user is dissatisfied. Negative issues dominate the review, even if a minor positive is mentioned.
*   **1 Star (Overwhelmingly Negative):** The product has critical flaws, is non-functional, or the user feels cheated. The user strongly warns others.

Review:
"{review}"

Analyze the review step-by-step (as described above) and then provide only the final numeric star rating. Your output must be a single line in the format "Rating: N".
