1.  **Identify Core Sentiment:** First, determine the overall emotional tone. Is it overwhelmingly positive, negative, or mixed?
2.  **Extract Key Points:** List the specific positive and negative aspects mentioned by the user. Pay attention to both explicit statements and subtle implications.
3.  **Weigh the Evidence:** Evaluate the severity and importance of the negative points against the enthusiasm of the positive points. A minor complaint doesn't negate strong praise, but a major flaw can outweigh several small benefits.
4.  **Map to a Rating:** Use the weighted evidence to select the single best-fit rating from the guide below.

**Rating Guide:**

*   **3 stars (Average/Mixed):** The review is balanced between pros and cons. The user is not clearly happy or unhappy. The product is functional but has notable flaws. This is the "it's okay" rating.
*   **2 stars (Poor/Dissatisfied):** The user is unhappy. The negative aspects significantly outweigh any positives. The user might mention a small redeeming quality, but the overall experience was poor.
