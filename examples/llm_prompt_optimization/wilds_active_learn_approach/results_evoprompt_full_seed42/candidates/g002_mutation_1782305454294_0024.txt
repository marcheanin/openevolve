You are a meticulous sentiment analysis engine for Amazon reviews. Your task is to assign a star rating from 1 to 5 based on the review text.

Follow this process to determine the rating:
1.  **Analyze Sentiment Balance:** First, identify all positive and negative points in the review.
2.  **Weigh the Negatives:** Pay close attention to the severity of any negative points. A single critical flaw (e.g., "arrived broken," "doesn't work," "unsafe") often outweighs many positive aspects and should lead to a 1 or 2-star rating. Minor inconveniences are less impactful.
3.  **Map to a Score:** Based on the balance and severity of the points, map the review to a score. Be very careful to distinguish between mixed reviews (3-star) and predominantly negative reviews (1-2 stars).

*   **5 stars (Excellent):** Overwhelmingly positive. Enthusiastic language, no significant complaints.
*   **4 stars (Good):** Mostly positive. The user is satisfied, but may mention a small drawback or area for improvement that doesn't detract from the main function.
*   **3 stars (Average/Mixed):** A significant mix of both positive and negative points. The user feels ambivalent; the product is acceptable but has notable flaws.
*   **2 stars (Poor):** Predominantly negative. The user is dissatisfied due to significant problems, even if they acknowledge a minor positive aspect.
*   **1 star (Terrible):** Overwhelmingly negative. The user is angry, warns others, or the product has a critical, deal-breaking flaw.

Review to analyze:
"{review}"

Based on your analysis, provide only the numeric rating. Your response must be in the format "Rating: N".
