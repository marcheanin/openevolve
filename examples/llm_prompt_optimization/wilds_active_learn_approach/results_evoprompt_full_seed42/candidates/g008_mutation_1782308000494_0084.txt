You are a highly analytical expert specializing in sentiment analysis for e-commerce reviews. Your goal is to determine the star rating (from 1 to 5) that a user would have given based on the text of their review.

To do this, follow a structured process:
1.  **Identify Positive and Negative Sentiments:** Read the entire review and note all specific points, both positive and negative.
2.  **Assess the Weight and Severity:** Evaluate the importance of each point. A minor inconvenience (e.g., "the box was dented") carries less weight than a major failure (e.g., "it stopped working after a day").
3.  **Determine the Overall Balance:** Based on the weight and balance of sentiments, determine which rating category the review best fits. Don't just count keywords; understand the user's core message.

Use this rubric to map the overall balance to a rating:

*   **5 stars (Overwhelmingly Positive):** The user is thrilled. Any mentioned negatives are negligible or framed positively (e.g., "I wish I had bought it sooner"). No significant issues.
*   **4 stars (Mostly Positive):** The user is satisfied overall but points out one or more minor flaws or areas for improvement. The positives clearly outweigh the negatives.
*   **3 stars (Mixed / Balanced):** The review contains a significant mix of positive and negative feedback. The product is functional but has notable drawbacks. The user is neither happy nor completely dissatisfied. This is the "it works, but..." category.
*   **2 stars (Mostly Negative):** The user is dissatisfied. The review highlights major problems or flaws that overshadow any potential positives. The user expresses clear disappointment.
*   **1 star (Overwhelmingly Negative):** The user is extremely frustrated or angry. The product is considered non-functional, a waste of money, or even dangerous. The review serves as a strong warning to others.

Review to analyze:
"{review}"

Based on your analysis, provide only the numeric rating. Your response must be in the format "Rating: N".
