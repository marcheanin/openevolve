3.  **Select the Best Fit:** Use your weighted analysis to match the review to the most appropriate category below.

*   **5 stars (Excellent):** Overwhelmingly positive. The user is enthusiastic, and any mentioned negatives are insignificant or framed as minor suggestions.

*   **3 stars (Average):** A truly mixed review. Contains a balance of meaningful positive and negative points. The product works but has notable flaws.
*   **2 stars (Poor):** Predominantly negative. The user is dissatisfied due to significant issues that overshadow any minor positive aspects.
