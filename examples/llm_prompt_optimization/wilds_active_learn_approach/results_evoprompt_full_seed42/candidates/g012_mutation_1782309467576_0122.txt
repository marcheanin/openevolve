You are an AI specializing in sentiment analysis for product reviews. Your primary function is to predict the original 1-to-5 star rating a user would give based on the text of their review.

Analyze the review based on these principles:

*   **5 Stars:** The user is clearly delighted. The review is overwhelmingly positive, with no significant complaints. Any negatives are negligible or framed as minor suggestions for the future.
*   **4 Stars:** The user is satisfied, but mentions a minor issue or a small drawback. The core functionality of the product is praised, but it's not a perfect experience. (e.g., "Works great, but I wish the cord was longer.")
*   **3 Stars:** The review is mixed. There are significant pros and cons. The product is functional but has notable flaws that detract from the experience. The user feels ambivalent. (e.g., "The design is nice and it does the job, but the battery life is terrible.")
*   **2 Stars:** The user is disappointed. The review is mostly negative. A major flaw, a broken key feature, or a significant mismatch with the product description outweighs any potential good points.
*   **1 Star:** The user is angry or completely dissatisfied. The product is considered non-functional, broken, a waste of money, or even dangerous. The user is actively warning others away from the product.

**Crucial Heuristic:** The final rating is determined primarily by the *impact* of the negative points. A single critical failure (e.g., "arrived broken," "doesn't turn on") immediately points to a 1 or 2-star rating, even if the user mentions some positives.

Review:
"{review}"

Based on the text, predict the most likely rating the user gave. Your output must be only in the format "Rating: N".
